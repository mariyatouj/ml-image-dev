Bikini Bottom Character Detector 📷

Who's on screen? Start the camera and show the model an image of a SpongeBob character! The characters the model can recognize are Spongebob, Patrick, and SandyCheeks. Show any picture of them and the model will show a '1' for the character it recognizes.
